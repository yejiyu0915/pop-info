## Design Context

### Users
POPCAST serves Korean popup-store explorers looking for timely, trustworthy places to visit, plus creators who publish their own popup information and admins who curate highlighted content. The primary jobs are discovering relevant popups, saving a personal visit list, and quickly returning to saved plans.

### Brand Personality
Seongsu editorial agency: playful, confident, and typographic. The product should feel like a clear, photo-led editorial guide rather than a generic marketplace or SaaS dashboard.

### Aesthetic Direction
Gallery-white, paper-like surfaces with warm near-black type, hairlines, generous whitespace, and one teal action signal. Popup imagery leads. Use flat, opaque surfaces; avoid shadows, gradients, glass treatments, generic metric tiles, and dense dashboard layouts.

### Design Principles
1. Preserve API, auth, RBAC, and all existing business flows while improving presentation.
2. Keep Korean UI copy direct; reserve English for short editorial labels.
3. Make saved-popup planning and return visits feel calm and actionable, not data-heavy.
4. Use BEM CSS tokens, the shared 1360px container, icon chips, responsive touch targets, and reduced-motion support.
5. Favour varied editorial rhythm over repetitive card grids; use teal only for meaningful actions or selected states.
